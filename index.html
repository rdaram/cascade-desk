01Event harvest

Events from the last 14 days, pulled from machine-readable feeds and targeted regional and trade-press searches. Every row links to its source. Cascade score 7+ = priority. Rows tagged mainstream are already front-page news, which lowers their edge.

02Priority cascade maps

How each 7+ event spreads, layer by layer: the event (L0), the industry hit first (L1), first stocks to move (L2), knock-on effects the market isn't pricing yet (L3), and feedback loops (L4). Click a box to expand it.

03Stock universe

US-listed names tied to the events, ranked by asymmetry score. Data is real (Yahoo Finance) and timestamped. Missing fields show as "n/a" and are never estimated. The Sandisk test is shown for reference.

04Trade calls (Tier 1 & Tier 2)

Every call has a written thesis, a way to be proven wrong, a kill switch and a time stop. Fills assume the worse side of the quote. Calls made after hours stay pending until real market quotes fill them.

05Watchlist: waiting for confirmation

Ideas that aren't tradable yet. Each one lists the exact observable trigger that would promote it, and what would drop it.

06Reflexive cascade monitor

Feedback loops in progress. The needle shows the stage, from Early through Accelerating and Mature to Breaking.

07Contrarian reversal scan

Winners where the cascade looks fully priced. Three checks: is the event resolving, is the stock up 40%+ since the event, and is options open interest crowded? Mirror trades are prepared but stay blocked while any check is open.

08Kill switch registry

One exit rule per active position, checked at every mark. Price triggers fire automatically. News triggers are flagged with python run.py kill ID "reason".

09Track record: every call

Nothing is deleted. Unfilled and cancelled calls stay visible. P&L is marked at the price you'd actually get on exit (sell at bid, buy back at ask) and includes $0.65/contract commissions.
