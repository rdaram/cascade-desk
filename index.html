Live prices

Prices stream straight from Finnhub to this browser (WebSocket trades for US stocks, free plan: up to 50 symbols, 60 REST calls a minute). Your key is stored only on this device (localStorage) and is sent only to finnhub.io. It is never published or sent anywhere else.

  1. Create a free account at finnhub.io/register.
  2. Copy your API key from finnhub.io/dashboard.
  3. Paste it below, then Test and Save.

. Labels: LIVE streaming trade with its time, Quote fresh snapshot, Delayed older than 2 minutes, Last close market closed (NYSE hours and holidays, ET).

Live view is informational. Official paper fills, stops and targets are booked only by the scheduled checks, from 5-minute bar data, so nothing is fabricated from a live tick.

Add to Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button .
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Swing Desk then opens full screen like an app, works offline with the last saved ledger, and keeps your live-price key on this device. The installed app has its own storage, so paste your Finnhub key once more inside it (gear icon).
